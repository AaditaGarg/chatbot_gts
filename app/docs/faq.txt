# GTS Customer Support Knowledge Base

## Account & Authentication

How do I reset my password?

To reset your password, go to the login page and click "Forgot password". Enter your registered email address and we will send a secure password reset link valid for 24 hours. If you do not receive the email within 5 minutes, check your spam folder or contact support@example.com.

How do I create an account?

Visit https://app.example.com/signup and provide your name, email, and a strong password (minimum 8 characters with one uppercase letter and one number). You will receive a verification email to activate your account.

How do I change my email address?

Go to Settings > Account > Email. Enter your new email and confirm with your current password. A verification link will be sent to the new address. Your old email remains active until verification is complete.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. This permanently removes all your data within 30 days per our data retention policy. Active subscriptions must be cancelled first. Contact support@example.com for assistance.

## Billing & Subscriptions

What is your refund policy?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for all new subscriptions. Contact support@example.com with your order ID to request a refund. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to your original payment method. Annual plans are eligible for a pro-rated refund within the first 30 days only.

What subscription plans do you offer?

GTS offers three plans:
- Basic ($9/month): 1 user, 5GB storage, email support
- Pro ($29/month): 5 users, 50GB storage, priority support, API access
- Enterprise (custom pricing): unlimited users, dedicated support, SLA guarantee
Visit https://example.com/pricing for full details.

How do I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Go to Settings > Billing > Change Plan. Upgrades take effect immediately with pro-rated billing. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing cycle. No refunds are issued for downgrades.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and bank transfer for Enterprise plans. All payments are processed securely via Stripe.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period. You can reactivate anytime before the period ends without losing your data.

## Product Features

What is GTS?

GTS (Global Tech Solutions) is a cloud-based project management and collaboration platform. It helps teams plan projects, track tasks, share files, and communicate in real time. GTS supports integrations with Slack, GitHub, Jira, and Google Workspace.

How do I invite team members?

Go to your workspace Settings > Members > Invite. Enter email addresses and select a role (Admin, Editor, or Viewer). Invited members receive an email with a join link valid for 7 days.

What integrations are available?

GTS integrates with: Slack (notifications), GitHub (issue sync), Jira (ticket sync), Google Drive (file storage), Microsoft Teams (notifications), and Zapier (custom workflows). Enable integrations in Settings > Integrations.

How do I export my data?

Go to Settings > Data > Export. Choose JSON or CSV format. Exports include projects, tasks, and comments. Large exports are emailed as a download link within 24 hours.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes. GTS is available on iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play). The mobile app supports task management, notifications, and file viewing. Offline mode is available for Pro and Enterprise plans.

## Technical Support

How do I contact support?

Email: support@example.com (response within 24 hours on Basic, 4 hours on Pro, 1 hour on Enterprise)
Live chat: Available Mon-Fri 9am-6pm EST on Pro and Enterprise plans
Phone: +1-800-555-0199 (Enterprise only)

What are your system requirements?

GTS is browser-based and works on Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Safari 14+, and Edge 90+. Minimum internet speed: 1 Mbps. Mobile apps require iOS 14+ or Android 10+.

The app is running slowly. What should I do?

Try these steps: 1) Clear your browser cache, 2) Disable browser extensions, 3) Check your internet connection, 4) Try a different browser. If issues persist, contact support@example.com with your browser version and a description of the problem.

How do I report a bug?

Go to Help > Report a Bug or email bugs@example.com. Include steps to reproduce, expected vs actual behavior, screenshots if possible, and your browser/OS version. Critical bugs are addressed within 48 hours.

## Security & Privacy

Is my data secure?

Yes. GTS uses AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. We are SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. Data is stored in AWS data centers in the US and EU. Enterprise customers can choose their data region.

Do you share my data with third parties?

We do not sell your data. We share data only with essential service providers (AWS for hosting, Stripe for payments) under strict data processing agreements. See our Privacy Policy at https://example.com/privacy.

How do I enable two-factor authentication (2FA)?

Go to Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy). Save your backup codes in a secure location. 2FA is required for all Admin accounts on Enterprise plans.
